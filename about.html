About NCBA&E

Our story, mission and values.

Our story

For over 31 years, NCBA&E has been shaping futures with academic excellence, innovation and purpose. Located in the heart of Lahore, in Gulberg, we bring business, science and arts education together.

Our mission is to give every student a rigorous education, hands-on experience and the confidence to lead.

What we value

Three principles guide every program we teach.

Academic excellence

Small classes, expert teachers and research-led learning.

Inclusion

A welcoming campus where students from every background can succeed.

Community impact

Projects with local partners that turn classroom ideas into real results.
